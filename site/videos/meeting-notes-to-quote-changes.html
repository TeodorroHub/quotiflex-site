Turn customer meeting notes into quote changes

Write the note the way you always do. With one click, the AI in Quotiflex suggests changes to the quote, updates to the deal brief and a task, each with the sentence it came from. The rules check every suggestion, and you decide what to apply.

Length 1:11Published 22 October 2026Watch on YouTube

What the video shows

  • Write the meeting note in the deal journal, next to the quote.
  • The AI reads the note and proposes quote changes: new lines, new quantities and product swaps, plus updates to the deal brief and a follow-up task.
  • Each suggestion shows the exact sentence it came from.
  • Your catalogue and pricing rules check every suggestion before anything changes.
  • Accepted changes are applied exactly like a manual edit, and the brief and the task are saved with the deal.

Never forget a machine accessory on a quote

Add a machine, and its usual accessories come with it. Every choice shows the price difference and the rule behind it. Take off something mandatory, and the quote shows it at once. To leave it out, you give a reason, and the reason stays with the quote under your name.

Length 1:26Published 22 October 2026Watch on YouTube

What the video shows

  • Add a machine to the quote by its name from the catalogue.
  • Its accessories come with it: required parts, defaults and options, all defined by your catalogue rules.
  • Every choice shows the price difference and the rule behind it: kept, recommended or optional.
  • Remove something required and the quote flags it as missing. It warns, it does not block.
  • Leave out a required item only with a reason. The reason is recorded on the quote with your name.

Bilingual quotation documents, nothing typed twice

The quote opens as the document your customer will read: two languages side by side, every machine with its description, pictures and price. What you sent stays exactly as you sent it. To change a text, click it and type; the prices come from the quote, so nobody types a wrong number.

Length 1:30Published 25 October 2026Watch on YouTube

What the video shows

  • The quotation document is built from the quote: cover letter, every machine with its description, pictures, technical data and price.
  • Two languages side by side, for example English and Danish, or one under the other, such as Chinese under English.
  • The solution from the deal brief goes into the cover letter in one click.
  • Edit any text in place. Prices stay locked, because they come from the quote.
  • Sent is sent: a presented revision keeps its prices, texts and exchange rate exactly as the customer saw them.

Quotiflex video · Rules editor

Change product rules without a developer

Describe a rule in one sentence, and the AI drafts it in your fields as a proposal. You check it, try it on a real quote and see every open quote it would change before anything is saved. Then the owner of every quote it touches is told, and updates it in one click.

Length 1:35Published 27 October 2026Watch on YouTube

What the video shows

  • Every rule in the catalogue reads as a sentence, with its type, effect, status and validity.
  • The product manager describes a new rule in one sentence, and the AI drafts it as a proposal: the trigger item, the target products, the rule type and the message to the sales manager.
  • A person checks every field, and the rule is tried on a real quote before it is saved. Nothing on the quote changes.
  • Before saving, the effect on all open quotes: which drafts would change, and which presented quotes stay as they are.
  • After saving, the owner of every affected quote is told what changes, and updates the quote to today's rules in one click, or not now.

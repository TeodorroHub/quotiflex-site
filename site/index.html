What does CPQ mean?

CPQ stands for Configure, Price, Quote: turning product requirements and commercial terms into a structured customer proposal.

Existing users can open the application with the Sign in button. To discuss access and your requirements, use the demo request below.

What should we cover in a demo?

Your product structure, pricing approach, approval process and how your team creates and revises proposals today.

How is pricing determined?

The right accessories, every time.

A few useful answers

Before we talk.

What does CPQ mean?

Configure, price, quote: from what the customer needs to a priced, approved document they can sign. Quotiflex keeps the deal around it in the same place: the notes, the tasks and the reason behind every decision.

How does the AI work, and who controls it?

The AI proposes and never writes on its own. Every proposal is marked, shows what it is based on and is checked by your rules. A person accepts it item by item. Administrators choose what is switched on, set a budget and see the cost of every call.

Do we need a developer to set it up?

No. Products, rules, approval levels, document templates and languages are settings. Rules can be written as a sentence, filled in as a table or imported from a spreadsheet, and tried on a quote before they are saved.

Does Quotiflex connect to the systems we already use?

Yes. Integration is built into the base of Quotiflex: which field goes where is a setting, every change for another system is delivered reliably, and products and customers keep the numbers of your systems. On that base we connect Quotiflex quickly to what your company runs, for example SAP, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Entra ID or Okta.

Can I access the Quotiflex application?

Existing users open the application with the Sign in button. To discuss access and your requirements, use the demo request below.

What should we cover in a demo?

Your machines and their accessories, how you price and approve discounts, the documents your customers expect, and how your team works on a deal today.

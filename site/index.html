Built for complex products

From configuration
to confident business decisions

Quotiflex combines product configuration, pricing, approval workflows and documentation in a single, easy-to-use platform.

  • Guided configuration. Rules decide which options fit together.
  • Pricing policy. Discount limits and margin floors on every line.
  • Approvals. A deal outside the policy goes to the right person.
  • Documents. PDF quotes and order acknowledgements from your templates.

How a quote moves

  1. 1

    Prepare

    Configure the products, add installation, training, travel and freight, and set the discounts.

  2. 2

    Approve

    When a deal is outside the policy, the right approver decides with the reasons in view.

  3. 3

    Present

    The revision is locked as the customer saw it: prices, texts and exchange rate are frozen.

  4. 4

    Revise

    Changes continue in the next revision letter. Every revision keeps its own link.

  5. 5

    Acknowledge

    The accepted revision becomes the order acknowledgement.

  • Guided configuration

    A selection wizard asks the right questions. Rules keep options that need or exclude each other in line, and table rules fill in the values.

  • Pricing rules and the Deal Desk

    Prices and costs with validity dates, discount limits and margin floors. The Deal Desk shows what a deal can still give and rounds the total within the policy.

  • Approvals

    Your approval rules decide who signs off which deal. The approver sees each reason, line by line, and every line price can be explained step by step.

  • Documents

    PDF quotes from your own templates, in one or two languages, showing as much price detail as you choose. Send them by email from the quote.

  • Quotes in several currencies

    Prices are kept in your base currency. Each quote has its own currency, and its exchange rate is frozen when the quote is presented.

  • Revisions and history

    Lettered revisions without limit, copies of earlier quotes and an audit trail of every change.

Solutions

One system for everyone who touches a quote

Each role sees the screens and the numbers it needs. There are also roles for translation editors and read-only viewers.

  • Sales managers

    Start from an opportunity, configure, price and present. The Deal Desk shows the room left in the policy before you ask for approval.

  • Product managers

    Keep the catalogue, prices, costs, rules and product texts in several languages, each with its own validity dates.

  • Approvers and finance

    Decide on deals outside the policy with margins, limits and reasons on one screen.

  • Administrators

    Set up users, roles and sales regions, the approval rules and the document templates.

About

Made for products with many options and firm rules

Quotiflex is a CPQ system (configure, price, quote) for manufacturers whose products are built to order. It is operated by LinkPort Systems s.r.o. in Prague, Czech Republic.

  • Every number explained

    Each line price can be traced step by step, from the list price to the total.

  • What was presented stays presented

    A presented revision is read-only. Changes continue in a new revision letter.

  • Each company apart

    Each company's data is kept apart from every other's in the database, and every change is recorded in an audit trail.
